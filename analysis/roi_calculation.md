# ROI Calculation

## Assumptions

For this example, we assume:

- 5,000 customer-support tickets per month
- Average manual handling time: 11.45 minutes per ticket
- Employee cost: ₹300 per hour
- AI automation reduces handling time by 40%
- Estimated AI implementation cost: ₹30,000

## Current Monthly Workload

5,000 tickets × 11.45 minutes

= 57,250 minutes

= approximately 954.17 hours per month

## Potential Time Saved

40% of 954.17 hours:

= approximately 381.67 hours saved per month

## Estimated Monthly Benefit

381.67 hours × ₹300/hour

= approximately ₹114,500 saved per month

## Estimated Annual Benefit

₹114,500 × 12

= ₹13,74,000 per year

## ROI

Implementation cost = ₹30,000

Annual benefit = ₹13,74,000

ROI:

(₹13,74,000 − ₹30,000) ÷ ₹30,000 × 100

= 4,480%

## Important Note

These figures are illustrative assumptions for a portfolio project.

They are not measured savings from a real company.

Actual ROI would depend on implementation cost, employee costs, automation accuracy, ticket volume, and the percentage of work that can actually be automated.
